[Question]
ID: ALG-001
Domain: Algebra
Type: MCQ
Difficulty: Medium

[Prompt]
The graphs of y = 2x + 1 and y = 11 - x intersect at the point (x, y). What is the value of x?

[Options]
A: 2
B: 10/3
C: 4
D: 10

[Correct Answer]
B

[Explanation]
At the intersection, the y-values are equal: 2x + 1 = 11 - x. Thus 3x = 10, so x = 10/3.

[Calculator Recommended]
Yes

[Desmos Entry]
Enter y=2x+1 and y=11-x. Select the intersection and read its x-coordinate.

[Hand Check]
Set the two expressions equal and solve: 2x+1=11-x.

[End Question]

[Question]
ID: ALG-002
Domain: Algebra
Type: MCQ
Difficulty: Hard

[Prompt]
For what value of k does the system y = 3x + 5 and y = 3x + k have infinitely many solutions?

[Options]
A: 0
B: 3
C: 5
D: 8

[Correct Answer]
C

[Explanation]
The equations have the same slope. They represent the same line only when their y-intercepts match, so k = 5.

[Calculator Recommended]
Yes

[Desmos Entry]
Use a slider for k, graph y=3x+5 and y=3x+k, and adjust k until the lines coincide.

[Hand Check]
Parallel lines have infinitely many common points only when both slope and intercept are identical.

[End Question]

[Question]
ID: ADV-001
Domain: Advanced Math
Type: MCQ
Difficulty: Medium

[Prompt]
What is the maximum value of f(x) = -2(x - 3)^2 + 18?

[Options]
A: -2
B: 3
C: 18
D: 36

[Correct Answer]
C

[Explanation]
The function is in vertex form. Its vertex is (3, 18), and because the coefficient of the squared term is negative, the vertex gives the maximum value: 18.

[Calculator Recommended]
Yes

[Desmos Entry]
Enter f(x)=-2(x-3)^2+18. Select the vertex on the graph.

[Hand Check]
Since (x-3)^2 is at least 0, the expression is largest when the squared term is 0.

[End Question]

[Question]
ID: ADV-002
Domain: Advanced Math
Type: MCQ
Difficulty: Hard

[Prompt]
The equation 2^x = 13 has solution x. Which value is closest to x?

[Options]
A: 2.7
B: 3.2
C: 3.7
D: 4.2

[Correct Answer]
B

[Explanation]
Because 2^3=8 and 2^4=16, x is between 3 and 4. Solving graphically or numerically gives x approximately 3.70, so C is correct.

[Calculator Recommended]
Yes

[Desmos Entry]
Graph y=2^x and y=13. Select their intersection; the x-coordinate is approximately 3.70.

[Hand Check]
The exact logarithmic form is x=log(13)/log(2)≈3.70.

[End Question]

Type: MCQ
Difficulty: Medium

[Prompt]
A line of best fit is y = 4.2x + 18, where x is the number of practice sessions and y is a predicted score. What does 4.2 represent?

[Options]
A: The predicted score after zero sessions
B: The number of sessions needed to increase the score by 4.2
C: The predicted score increase for each additional session
D: The total score after 4.2 sessions

[Correct Answer]
C

[Explanation]
The slope is 4.2 score points per practice session. The intercept, 18, is the predicted score at zero sessions.

[Calculator Recommended]
Yes

[Desmos Entry]
Enter y=4.2x+18 and inspect the slope or use a table to compare consecutive x-values.

[Hand Check]
In y=mx+b, m is the change in y for a one-unit increase in x.

[Prompt]
A right triangle has an angle θ with opposite side 9 and hypotenuse 15. What is sin(θ)?

[Options]
A: 3/5
B: 5/3
C: 3/2
D: 2/3

[Correct Answer]
A

[Explanation]
Sine equals opposite divided by hypotenuse: sin(θ)=9/15=3/5.

[Hand Check]

[Question]
ID: GEO-002
Domain: Geometry and Trigonometry
Type: MCQ
Difficulty: Hard

[Prompt]
What is the radius of the circle represented by (x - 2)^2 + (y + 5)^2 = 49?

[Options]
A: 2
B: 5
C: 7
D: 49

[Correct Answer]
C

[Explanation]
Circle form is (x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2. Here r^2=49, so r=7.

[Calculator Recommended]
Yes

[Desmos Entry]
Enter (x-2)^2+(y+5)^2=49 and inspect the graph or use the circle equation form.

[Hand Check]
Take the positive square root of 49 because radius is positive.

[End Question]

[Question]
ID: MIX-002
Domain: Cross-Domain
Type: MCQ
Difficulty: Hard

[Prompt]
In a survey, 60% of 40 students prefer option A. Of those students, 25% also prefer option B. How many students prefer both A and B?

[Options]
A: 4
B: 6
C: 10
D: 15

[Correct Answer]
B

[Explanation]
First find the A group: 0.60(40)=24. Then find 25% of that group: 0.25(24)=6.

[Calculator Recommended]
Yes

[Desmos Entry]
Enter 0.25*(0.60*40).

[Hand Check]
Apply the conditional percentage to the restricted A group, not to all 40 students.

[End Question]
